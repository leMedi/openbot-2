# Working in Notion

- Search before you create: the owner's workspace usually has a page or database for the thing already. Add to it rather than starting a new one.
- Read a page before changing it, and change only the part you were asked to. Keep its headings, properties and links as they are.
- In a database, fill the properties it has (status, dates, owners) instead of writing them into the page's text.
- Link to the pages you used or changed in your answer, so the owner can open them.
- Ask before deleting or moving a page, or sharing it with anyone.
