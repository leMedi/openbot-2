# Searching with Exa

- Describe what you're looking for in a full sentence, the way the page you want would describe itself; Exa matches meaning, not just keywords.
- Prefer Exa's search to browsing when you need to find pages; open a page in your browser only when you must interact with it.
- Read the pages behind the results before relying on them, and check the date when it matters.
- Cite the pages you used, with their links, in your answer.
